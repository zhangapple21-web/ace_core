# ACE Teacher Review Packet v1

ACE now has two deliberately separate paths:

* **PRODUCTION_ADMISSION** remains unchanged. It still requires the existing
  quote/1m/index data-health, lineage, freshness, coverage, completeness and
  independent cross-source checks.
* **TEACHER_REVIEW_RESEARCH** can prepare at most two cards from a fresh,
  traceable session or at least three complete recent sessions. Cards remain
  `RESEARCH_ONLY` and require the teacher's confirmation.

Each card carries its observation time, thesis, confirmation trigger,
invalidation condition and source references. Missing hard evidence produces
an empty packet rather than a fabricated recommendation. The packet owns no
Advisor, Risk, Telegram, broker or order integration.
